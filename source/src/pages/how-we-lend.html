From enquiry to disbursement

The path from application to disbursement, the standards applied along the way, and the safeguards around them.

Your deadline matters

We handle complete files promptly and work towards your target date. Review time runs during office hours. Final approval and disbursement remain subject to verification, accepted terms and completion of all conditions.

B

Eligibility & documents

Assessment moves faster when the file is complete. These lists are the usual starting point; more may be requested depending on the product and amount.

Who is eligible

  • Individuals and enterprises operating in Uganda
  • Salaried clients with verifiable income
  • Micro and small business owners with a productive need
  • Sole proprietors, companies and registered organisations
  • Clients able to demonstrate capacity to repay
  • Clients who can provide security where the facility requires it
  • Applicants across urban, semi‑urban and rural Uganda

C

Credit assessment

Every file is measured against the same five standards, in the same order, whoever the applicant is.

Free access to service

No fees or commissions are required to be served. Enquiries, application and credit assessment are free.

Every fee has a schedule

Every applicable loan charge is disclosed on a written schedule and accepted before commitment.

4-hour review target

We aim to review complete files within 4 working hours. We prioritise your target date and keep you informed.
