What this website does with your details

The Apply and Contact forms

The details you type stay on your device until you choose to send them. When the site is connected to Biashara’s form service, pressing send delivers your file to Biashara over an encrypted connection. Otherwise the site opens your own email or WhatsApp with the file written out, and nothing is sent until you press send there. Those messages are then handled by your email or WhatsApp provider as usual.

Saved in your browser

While you fill in the Apply form, your answers are kept in this browser’s local storage so you can come back to them. They are not sent anywhere when saved. Choose Start again on the Apply page, or clear this site’s data in your browser, to remove them.

The site also remembers one display setting on your device: whether high contrast is on.

No tracking

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies and loads no third‑party trackers. Fonts, scripts and images are served from the site itself. Links to Google Maps and WhatsApp open those services, which have their own privacy terms.
