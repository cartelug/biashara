How charges are communicated

  1. 01

    Interest is quoted per month

    2.8% per month applies across all four products, calculated on the approved principal.

  2. 02

    No hidden charges

    Every applicable loan charge appears on a written schedule. Nothing is charged without your prior acceptance.

  3. 03

    Free access to service

    No fees or commissions are required to be served. Enquiries, applications and credit assessment are free.

  4. 04

    You accept before signing

    Interest, any processing fee and other loan charges are explained and accepted before you commit.

All charges scheduled & accepted

We have no hidden charges. Every applicable loan fee, including any processing fee, is listed on a written schedule and applies only after you accept it. Access to service, application and credit assessment require no fees or commissions.
