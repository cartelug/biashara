About Biashara

Biashara Trading Company Limited was incorporated in 2014 to provide accessible, short‑term bridge financing for the growing Ugandan market.

Origin and approach

The company started as an SME focused on faster access to credit in a convenient, client‑friendly environment.

Its ambition is to develop from a money‑lending business into a microfinance institution and, over time, a broader financial‑services provider.

That ambition has not changed how it works day to day. Speed is combined with transparent communication, disciplined assessment and personal service — the same file standards applied to every client, whoever they are.

Values

Our core values

Five commitments that decide how a file is handled — before, during and after approval.

01Clients first

We listen carefully, respond promptly and build long‑term relationships through useful service.

02Transparency

We communicate approved terms, charges and expectations clearly, in writing, before any commitment is made.

03Responsible lending

We assess ability to repay and structure facilities around an approved need — not the largest amount a client could technically borrow.

04Professional service

We work with discipline, accountability, teamwork and respect, and hold ourselves to the standards we ask of clients.

05Sustainable growth

We support practical financial decisions that strengthen the client and the company over time.

Operating objectives

Biashara’s operating objectives combine client access with a sound credit culture: careful assessment, low delinquency and sustainable operations. The company serves eligible clients across urban, semi‑urban and rural Uganda, subject to its operating capacity and approved requirements.
