                 Biashara Trading Company Limited was incorporated in 2014 to

               CREDIT
               ASSESSMENT
               Every file is measured against the same five standards, in the
               same order, whoever the applicant is.


C1             Character               Credit history, conduct and demonstrated willingness to meet obligations.


C2             Capacity                Cash flow and demonstrated ability to repay within the approved tenure.


C3             Capital                 The client’s own financial position and commitment to the stated purpose.


C4             Collateral              Acceptable security where the approved facility requires it.


C5             Conditions              Purpose, tenure, sector and the wider context of the request.
