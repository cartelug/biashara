Executive summary

Biashara Trading Company Limited is a Kampala‑based lender providing short‑term bridge financing to eligible individuals and enterprises across Uganda. Incorporated in 2014, the company helps clients close temporary cash‑flow gaps without unnecessary delay. Four products — school fees, salary, asset finance and business loans — share one published range, one interest rate and a maximum six‑month term. Every facility is assessed responsibly, with charges disclosed and accepted before commitment.

Incorporated2014Kampala, Uganda
Facility range1–50MUganda shillings
Interest2.8%Per month, all products
Maximum term6 monthsShort‑term by design

Speed is only valuable when it is paired with discipline. Every facility Biashara approves is one it believes the client can comfortably repay.

What the company doesShort‑term financing and business solutions built around transparency, reliability and efficient processing.
Who it servesEligible individuals and enterprises that need practical access to credit without unnecessary delay.
What guides every decisionA five‑point credit assessment — character, capacity, capital, collateral and conditions — applied to every file, in the same order.

Part One · The company · p. 07

02 · About Biashara

Biashara Trading Company Limited was incorporated in 2014 to provide accessible, short‑term bridge financing for the growing Ugandan market.

The company started as an SME focused on faster access to credit in a convenient, client‑friendly environment. Its ambition is to develop from a money‑lending business into a microfinance institution and, over time, a broader financial‑services provider.

That ambition has not changed how it works day to day. Speed is combined with transparent communication, disciplined assessment and personal service — the same file standards applied to every client, whoever they are.

The ambition, in order

  1. Money‑lending business — officially incorporated in 2014
  2. Microfinance institution — the stated vision
  3. Financial‑services provider — broader, over time
01 · A practical startAn SME built to give faster access to credit in a client‑friendly environment.

Our main purpose is short‑term bridge financing for temporary cash‑flow gaps. Four products support education, salary, asset and business needs, with a maximum term of six months.

01 · School Fees LoansShort‑term finance for eligible clients to meet school‑fee obligations and manage education cash flow across a term.

Part Three · How we work · p. 19

11 · How we lend

A four‑step path. Every application follows the same sequence, whoever the applicant is.

01 · Enquire & applyBegin your loan request by email: inquiries@biasharafinance.com. We guide your application and required documents.
02 · AssessComplete files: review target within 4 working hours. We check purpose, affordability and required security.
03 · Approve & acceptApproved terms, interest and any loan charges are scheduled in writing. You accept them before signing.
04 · DisburseFunds are released once verification, accepted terms and all conditions are complete.

Your deadline matters. We handle complete files promptly and work towards your target date. Review time runs during office hours. Final approval and disbursement remain subject to verification, accepted terms and completion of all conditions.

Part Three · How we work · p. 20

12 · Eligibility & documents

Assessment moves faster when the file is complete. These lists are the usual starting point; more may be requested depending on the product and amount.

Who is eligible

  • Individuals and enterprises operating in Uganda
  • Salaried clients with verifiable income
  • Micro and small business owners with a productive need
  • Sole proprietors, companies and registered organisations
  • Clients able to demonstrate capacity to repay
  • Clients who can provide security where the facility requires it
  • Applicants across urban, semi‑urban and rural Uganda

Documents usually requested

  • Valid national identification
  • Proof of income — payslips or business records
  • Bank or mobile‑money statements for recent months
  • Business registration documents, where applicable
  • Evidence of the purpose — invoice, fee structure or quotation
  • Security documentation, where the facility requires it
  • Contact and referee details
A note on honestyAccurate information speeds up a decision and protects you from taking on a facility you cannot comfortably service.
If documents are missingCome in anyway. The team will tell you exactly what is outstanding before the file goes to assessment.

Part Three · How we work · p. 21

13 · Credit assessment

Every file is measured against the same five standards, in the same order, whoever the applicant is.

C1 · CharacterCredit history, conduct and demonstrated willingness to meet obligations.
C2 · CapacityCash flow and demonstrated ability to repay within the approved tenure.
C3 · CapitalThe client’s own financial position and commitment to the stated purpose.
C4 · CollateralAcceptable security where the approved facility requires it.
C5 · ConditionsPurpose, tenure, sector and the wider context of the request.
Free access to serviceNo fees or commissions are required to be served. Enquiries, application and credit assessment are free.
Every fee has a scheduleEvery applicable loan charge is disclosed on a written schedule and accepted before commitment.
4‑hour review targetWe aim to review complete files within 4 working hours. We prioritise your target date and keep you informed.

Office hours apply. Final approval and disbursement follow all required checks and conditions.
