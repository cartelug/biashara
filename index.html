Process

How we lend

A four‑step path. Every application follows the same sequence, whoever the applicant is.

  1. 1

    Step 01

    Enquire & apply

    Begin your loan request by email: inquiries@biasharafinance.com. We guide your application and required documents.

  2. 2

    Step 02

    Assess

    Complete files: review target within 4 working hours.3 We check purpose, affordability and required security.

  3. 3

    Step 03

    Approve & accept

    Approved terms, interest and any loan charges are scheduled in writing. You accept them before signing.

  4. 4

    Step 04

    Disburse

    Funds are released once verification, accepted terms and all conditions are complete.

Standards

Credit assessment

Every file is measured against the same five standards, in the same order, whoever the applicant is.

  1. C1

    Character

    Credit history, conduct and demonstrated willingness to meet obligations.

  2. C2

    Capacity

    Cash flow and demonstrated ability to repay within the approved tenure.

  3. C3

    Capital

    The client’s own financial position and commitment to the stated purpose.

  4. C4

    Collateral

    Acceptable security where the approved facility requires it.

  5. C5

    Conditions

    Purpose, tenure, sector and the wider context of the request.

Every file reviewed before approval
