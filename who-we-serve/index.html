Urban · Semi‑urban · Rural

06

Who we serve

Eligible clients across urban, semi‑urban and rural Uganda. All facilities remain subject to assessment and approval.

Where we work

Urban, semi‑urban and rural Uganda, served from a single Kampala office so that decisions stay close to the people who make them.

The four groups

  1. 01

    Entrepreneurs

    Micro and small business owners with viable productive needs.

    Business Loans

  2. 02

    Salaried clients

    Eligible employees with a sound ability to meet approved repayment requirements.

    Salary Loans

  3. 03

    Companies & NGOs

    Organisations seeking approved short‑term financial solutions.

    Business Loans

  4. 04

    Growing businesses

    Sole proprietors and companies financing stock, cash flow or productive assets.

    Asset Financing

All facilities remain subject to assessment and approval

Eligibility is assessed on repayment capacity, purpose and security where required — not on the category a client falls into.1
